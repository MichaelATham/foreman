# Example briefs

Paste any of these into the UI, or run them headless:

```bash
foreman "@examples/bracket.txt" --out out/bracket
```

**bracket.txt** — A mounting bracket: 80 × 50 × 8 mm plate with a central boss Ø20 × 20 mm
tall carrying a Ø10 through-hole, four Ø5.5 mounting holes 8 mm from the corners, 4 mm
outer fillets and 2 mm fillets where the boss meets the plate.

**enclosure.txt** — A hollow enclosure 90 × 60 × 30 mm with 2 mm walls, open on top, with
four Ø3.2 screw bosses (Ø7) in the inside corners reaching the top rim, and 3 mm rounded
vertical outside edges.

**pulley.txt** — A pulley for a 6 mm round belt: Ø60 outer, 12 mm wide, V-groove 4 mm deep,
Ø8 bore with a 2.5 mm keyway, hub Ø20 extending 6 mm on one side.

**pillow_block.txt** — A pillow block bearing housing for a 22 mm bearing OD, 35 mm centre
height, base 70 × 30 mm with two Ø6.5 mounting holes, 3 mm fillets.

**mcu_case.txt** — A two-part 3D-printed microcontroller case: base plus a lid held on
by snap tabs. Exercises the assembly fit checks (tabs on the right side, lid sized to the
case, interference only at the tabs).

**hex_nut.txt** — A hex nut M10: 17 mm across flats, 8 mm thick, Ø8.5 hole, chamfered
outer edges.

Follow-ups worth trying after a part is verified:

* "Make the mounting holes M6 clearance (Ø6.6) and counterbore them Ø11 × 6 deep."
* "Shell the whole thing to 2 mm walls from below."
* "Add a rib from the boss to each short side, 3 mm thick, full height of the boss."
